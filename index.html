Who I Am

I'm Lawrence Nyongesa — a DevOps Engineer and Cloud Specialist with production experience designing and operating Kubernetes environments on AWS, Azure (AKS), and GCP (GKE). I automate infrastructure with Terraform, Ansible, and Packer, and build CI/CD pipelines with GitHub Actions, Jenkins, and GitLab CI/CD.

On the operations side, I work with Prometheus and Grafana for monitoring, and with RBAC, TLS/mTLS, and HashiCorp Vault for security and secrets management. My background also includes systems and network administration — Linux, Proxmox, VMware, and server hardening.

Alongside infrastructure work, I have experience in AI/ML data operations — data annotation, image labeling, multilingual language labeling, and audio transcription — and I serve as a bioinformatics subject-matter expert, supporting scientific data review, validation, and quality assurance for specialized AI/ML datasets.
